# 🛒 Smart Integrated Retail Management System

An all-in-one web-based retail management application designed to streamline inventory tracking, sales processing, credit control, and customer management for retail businesses.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **📊 Interactive Dashboard:** Real-time metrics for daily sales, stock synchronization, and critical inventory alerts.
* **💳 Automated Credit Control:** Real-time tracking of customer credit limits, outstanding balances, and payment terms to minimize financial risks.
* **🧾 Order History & Invoicing:** Seamless log of past transactions with instant, downloadable PDF invoice generation.
* **👥 Customer Management:** Centralized hub for managing customer profiles, credit terms, and purchase histories.
* **📦 Inventory Synchronization:** Real-time stock updates to prevent stock-outs and track product movement.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend & Backend:** C# / ASP.NET Web Forms
* **Database:** Microsoft SQL Server
* **Libraries & Tools:** iTextSharp (PDF Generation), Visual Studio 2022
